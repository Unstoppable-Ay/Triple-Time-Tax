Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use Triple Time Tax services.

1. Information We Collect

  • Personal Information: Name, email, phone number, address, and tax-related details.
  • Automatically Collected Information: IP addresses, browser type, device type, cookies, usage data.
  • Financial Information: Payment or transaction data for tax filing services.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve our tax filing services.
  • To communicate with you regarding your account or filings.
  • To comply with legal and regulatory requirements.
  • To personalize your experience on our website.

3. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. Data may only be shared with:

  • Authorized tax authorities when legally required.
  • Trusted service providers assisting in payments or hosting services.

4. Data Security

We use strict security measures, including encryption, secure servers, and access controls to protect your information.

5. Your Rights

  • Access and correct your personal data.
  • Request deletion of your personal data.
  • Opt-out of marketing communications.

6. Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies to enhance user experience, remember preferences, and analyze traffic. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but some features may not work properly.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. Changes will be posted on this page with the date of the latest revision.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about your privacy, contact us at:

  • Email: support@triple-timetax.com
  • Phone: +234 800 123 4567
